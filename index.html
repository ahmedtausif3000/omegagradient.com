FREQUENTLY ASKED

GPU sourcing, answered.

Q.01

What is Omega Gradient?

Omega Gradient is a GPU supply sourcing and intelligence desk for reserved clusters and on-demand compute. We shape the requirement, scan provider options, normalize technical and commercial terms, and qualify the path to delivery. Public pricing data supports the comparison; private supply and buyer diligence remain confidential.

Q.02

How fast can I get GPUs?

For reserved blocks and larger clusters, timing depends on whether capacity is live, allocated, being installed, or under development. Omega Gradient makes that evidence status explicit. For flexible needs, on-demand instances can deploy from the live marketplace when inventory is available.

Q.03

How does Omega Gradient pricing work?

We source capacity through operators and infrastructure partners. The contracting, billing and support structure depends on the provider and deployment and is set out in the proposal. Marketplace listings show hourly pricing and use prepaid credits. Our dated price index provides public-rate context; reserved quotes depend on the system, term and delivery conditions.

Q.04

Which GPUs can Omega Gradient source?

We help source Hopper and Blackwell capacity, including H100, H200, B200, B300, GB200 and GB300 systems. The right option depends on your region, start date, term and workload. The supplier pricing report shows dated public rates; the marketplace shows its current catalog.

Q.05

What do H100, H200 and B200 GPUs cost per hour?

In the public price snapshot assembled 2026-11-07 (source observations 2026-11-02 – 2026-11-07), median provider-average rates were H100 $2.85, H200 $4.31, B300 $9.50, GB300 $15.72, A100 $2.18 and L40S $1.73 per GPU-hour; B200 had no included public rate. These are dated public list-price observations across the monitored registry, not quotes or allocations. Min, median and max by GPU family, with source observation dates, are on the dated price index; reserved cluster pricing is quoted per system, term and delivery condition.
