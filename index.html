FREQUENTLY ASKED

GPU sourcing, answered.

Q.01

What is Omega Gradient?

Omega Gradient is a GPU supply sourcing and intelligence desk for reserved clusters and on-demand compute. We shape the requirement, scan provider options, normalize technical and commercial terms, and qualify the path to delivery. Public pricing data supports the comparison; private supply and buyer diligence remain confidential.

Q.02

How fast can I get GPUs?

For reserved blocks and larger clusters, timing depends on whether capacity is live, allocated, being installed, or under development. Omega Gradient makes that evidence status explicit. For flexible needs, on-demand instances can deploy from the live marketplace when inventory is available.

Q.03

How does Omega Gradient pricing work?

The sourcing desk resells capacity from many suppliers instead of promoting its own datacenters. Marketplace listings show one all-in hourly price up front — no egress surprises, billed only while the instance exists. Reserved cluster quotes are benchmarked against our continuously refreshed GPU price index, so you can see how any quote compares to the market before committing.

Q.04

Which GPUs can Omega Gradient source?

Our public price index tracks H100, H200, B200, B300, GB300, A100, L40S, and RTX 5090, and the on-demand marketplace lists dozens more models. As of July 2026, market-median rates include H100 at ~$2.50/GPU-hr and H200 at ~$3.07/GPU-hr — see the full supplier pricing report for provider-level rates.
