Founded by Tausif Ahmed

Tausif Ahmed is the Founder & CEO of Omega Gradient. He works with buyers on their current and upcoming compute needs, and with providers on available capacity and future deployments.

Have a requirement or capacity coming online? Get in touch or share your GPU requirements.

How we help

  • Source suitable clusters. Start with GPU type and count, region, target start, term and the requirements that matter to your workload.
  • Compare the full offer. Look at specifications, pricing, payment terms, provider responsibilities and the path to delivery together.
  • Keep the evidence clear. Distinguish operating capacity, future allocations, provider representations and questions that remain open.

Who provides the infrastructure?

The infrastructure is operated by third-party providers. Reserved opportunities can use different contracting and commercial arrangements; the proposal should identify the contracting entity, operator, billing party and support responsibilities for that deployment.

The on-demand marketplace is a separate, prepaid compute flow. Availability and deployment depend on the selected configuration and provider. Public market research helps with comparison; a public price or supply signal is not a reservation.
