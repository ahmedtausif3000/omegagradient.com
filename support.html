Contact & support

Tell us what you need help with.

Email hello@omegagradient.com for GPU sourcing, marketplace questions or a problem with your account.

For new capacity requirements, share your GPU requirements. Include GPU type and count, location, timing, term and any workload constraints you already know.

Marketplace access or deployment

Include your account email, instance ID if you have one, the approximate time and time zone, and the error message. A screenshot can help if it does not expose credentials or private workload data.

  • Sign-in: use the email address associated with your account. If the code does not arrive, check spam and note when you requested it.
  • Provisioning or access: describe whether the instance is provisioning, running or showing an error, and whether SSH or the workload itself is affected.
  • Ending a run: terminate the instance in the console and check its final status. Closing a browser tab or disconnecting SSH does not terminate compute.

Billing or credit questions

Include your account email, receipt or payment reference, date, amount and any related instance ID. Review your balance, billing history and auto-top-up setting in the console.

A support email is not an immediate termination command. If you need to end compute usage, use the console and include any failure to terminate in your message.

Security, abuse or privacy

Use the same contact address with “Security”, “Abuse” or “Privacy” in the subject. Describe the affected page or resource, relevant time and the minimum information needed to understand the issue.

Keep credentials private. Do not email a password, sign-in code, SSH private key, full card number or unredacted identity document. Start with a description so an appropriate way to exchange sensitive information can be agreed.

Response and service commitments

This contact page does not promise a response time or round-the-clock support. Any agreed support hours, response targets or service remedies belong in the applicable order or service agreement.
