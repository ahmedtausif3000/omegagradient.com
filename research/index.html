Research · dated market evidence

Reserved GPU market research and public datasets.

Omega Gradient publishes anonymized, sample-safe reads of the reserved GPU market alongside the dated public datasets behind its price index. Every figure carries its observation or assembly date; nothing here is a quote or an allocation.

Index published November 4, 2026By Tausif Ahmed, Founder & CEO

Research editions

  • Reserved GPU Market Report — Q3 2026 early read (published August 21, 2026; 50 eligible observations, July 8 to August 21, 2026). Platform mix, start windows, block-size bands, evidence freshness and quote-field completeness for represented reserved supply. Aggregate data: reserved_gpu_market_report_2026_q3.json.

New editions are published when a fresh sample clears the methodology thresholds (minimum public cell of five observations, dated evidence, no provider or buyer identities). Subscribe to the update feed to be notified.

Public datasets

  • Cloud GPU Price Index: min, median and max USD per GPU-hour by GPU family from evidence-verified public rate rows, with per-GPU pages. JSON: public_gpu_market.json.
  • GPU supplier pricing report: named-provider public list prices with source links, confidence and observation dates.
  • Marketplace availability snapshot: provider-anonymous listings observed in the dated catalog sample. Live JSON: /api/market/catalog.

Method and limits

Observations are dated public-market or sourcing-desk records, not confirmed allocations. The diligence framework behind the reserved research is described in How we qualify reserved GPU clusters; the buyer-side checklist is the procurement checklist. Machine-readable summaries for AI agents: llms.txt and llms-full.txt.
