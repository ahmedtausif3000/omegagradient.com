02 / Methodology

What each record tells you.

01

Public routes, distinct systems

One record represents a provider and GPU product route. Regional options are kept in the same record. We include exact product names and documented reservation paths; generic hardware announcements and private tracker data are excluded.

02

Evidence has a boundary

Provider documentation establishes what a provider describes. It does not prove free powered-on quantity, a contiguous fabric, a current price or delivery. Unknown fields stay unknown. The pilot includes no confirmed Omega Gradient allocation.

03

Dates you can audit

“Source checked” is the documentation review date, not an inventory verification. Records have a 30-day review window. After the due date, they are marked “Review overdue” until a new source review; a rebuild never renews evidence dates. The displayed range covers the earliest and latest route reviews. In the JSON download, checked_at is the earliest route review and review_due is the earliest deadline; each row remains authoritative. Reviewing a shared source page does not renew every route.

04

Qualify before committing

Confirm the contracting entity, exact site, allocation controller, usable GPU count, fabric, storage, start window, term and acceptance plan. Omega Gradient helps turn that evidence into comparable options. Public inclusion does not imply a commercial relationship.

Edition 2026-11-12 · Review owner: Omega Gradient Intelligence. The pilot is a selected reference set, not a market census or provider ranking. Source publication dates were not consistently supplied; we report the review date instead. Provider descriptions remain the responsibility of their publishers.
