03 / How to read this

A comparison is only as complete as its terms.

Term expenses

GPU count × reserved hours × GPU-hour rate, plus one-time fees and monthly extras. Prepaid compute is already part of this amount. A refundable deposit ties up cash but is not counted as an expense.

Cash due at signing

The chosen compute payment, one-time fees due at start, any recurring fees payable in advance, and the refundable deposit. “First month” is capped at the full term for shorter reservations. An advance payment is not added to term expenses again.

What still needs diligence

Equal prices do not mean equal delivery. Confirm allocation control, exact SKU, contiguous fabric, site, start/end times, support, acceptance, and remedies. Taxes, financing, currency changes, overages, and exit charges are outside this calculation unless included in your entered fees.

Per-GPU-hour cost divides term expenses by all reserved GPU-hours. Utilization scenarios divide by useful GPU-hours instead; they are sensitivity calculations, not performance forecasts. Unknown costs remain unresolved, so a known subtotal is not a complete quote or a spending ceiling.
