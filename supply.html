Frequently Asked

Selling GPU capacity on Omega Gradient

How do I start selling GPU capacity?

Three steps: describe your machine (GPU model, count, specs, region, and your hourly ask), run one command to install the lightweight agent, and earn per rented hour. The agent verifies your hardware with nvidia-smi and works behind NAT — no ports to open, no inbound firewall rules.

How much do I earn when my GPU is rented?

You earn your full hourly ask for every rented hour — you set the price when you list the machine, and your payout is never reduced. Buyers see a final listed price upfront with no hidden fees; the listing form shows both your ask and the buyer price before you publish.

Do buyers see who I am?

No. Listings are provider-anonymous: buyers see verified specs, region, and price — never your identity. When someone rents your machine, the agent grants them SSH access automatically and revokes it when they're done.

What hardware can I list?

Anything from a single idle workstation GPU to full datacenter racks. Individuals, prosumers, and datacenters register through the same flow; each machine's GPU model and count are verified by the agent before it goes live. Curious what rates the market bears?
