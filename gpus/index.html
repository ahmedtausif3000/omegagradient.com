Methodology

USD per GPU-hour; instance-hour prices are divided by gpu_count when gpu_count is known.

PRICING BASIS

Provider-normalized average: average SKUs/offers within each provider and GPU family first, then average provider means so one marketplace does not dominate.

AVERAGING METHOD

Every included price row must include an evidence URL, pass the evidence-confidence threshold, use a supported GPU-hour normalization path, and stay within a GPU-family sanity envelope before it is included in averages.

VERIFICATION METHOD

Candidate prices outside GPU-family USD/GPU-hour sanity floors or ceilings are quarantined from averages and the public feed until manually verified.
